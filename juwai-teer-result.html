Juwai Teer Afternoon Timings & Scoring

Juwai Teer, organized in the West Jaintia Hills district of Meghalaya, is the earliest afternoon Teer result declared each day. Because its First Round (FR) is announced around 2:15 PM IST and Second Round (SR) around 3:00 PM IST, it sets the pace for the rest of the day's archery results.

The scoring follows the standard Meghalaya Teer format: archers shoot at a traditional bamboo target, officials count the embedded arrows, and the last two digits of the total count determine the winning two-digit number from 00 to 99. Matches run Monday through Saturday.

Many regular followers compare Juwai Teer's afternoon numbers with Shillong and Khanapara results later in the day, using the previous results archive to keep track of weekly trends.
