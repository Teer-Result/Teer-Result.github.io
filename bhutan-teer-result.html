Bhutan Teer Daily Results & Game Format

Bhutan Teer is an afternoon archery-based number game that follows the familiar two-round structure used across the region. Results are declared daily in two stages: the First Round (FR) at 4:00 PM and the Second Round (SR) at 5:00 PM.

Each round produces a two-digit number between 00 and 99 based on the final count of arrows hitting the target. Players who miss the live announcement can check the updated scoreboard here or browse the historical chart to verify past dates.

For accurate tracking, always cross-check the date and round label (FR vs. SR) before recording any numbers for your personal reference.
