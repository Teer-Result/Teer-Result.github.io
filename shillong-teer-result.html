Shillong Teer Timings, Rules & History

Shillong Teer is one of the oldest legal archery-based games in Meghalaya, India, organized daily from Monday to Saturday at the Polo Ground in Shillong by the Khasi Hills Archery Sports Association (KHASA). Twelve local archery clubs participate under the Meghalaya Amusements and Betting Tax (Amendment) Act, making it a regulated cultural and sporting tradition.

Every afternoon, 50 archers gather and shoot 30 arrows each in the First Round (FR) at a cylindrical bamboo target. Once the shooting concludes, officials count all arrows that successfully stick into the target. The last two digits of the total arrow count become the official First Round result, declared around 3:45 PM to 4:00 PM IST. After a short break, the Second Round (SR) takes place with 20 arrows per archer, and the result is declared around 4:45 PM to 5:00 PM IST.

Players often review the previous 7 to 30 days of Shillong Teer results to spot recurring house and ending digits. Sundays are always closed for Teer matches.
