Khanapara Teer Schedule, Format & Archive

Khanapara Teer is played near the Meghalaya–Assam border in Ri-Bhoi district and is widely followed across Guwahati and Shillong. Like Shillong Teer, it operates six days a week from Monday to Saturday, remaining closed on Sundays and major public holidays.

In the First Round (FR), archers shoot their allotted arrows at the bamboo target, and the last two digits of the total hit count are announced at approximately 4:05 PM IST. The Second Round (SR) follows shortly after, with results declared around 4:50 PM IST. Because Khanapara Teer timings are close to Shillong Teer, many players track both scoreboards together.

Checking the weekly Khanapara Teer previous results chart helps players verify past FR and SR pairs and understand recent number patterns without relying on guesswork.
