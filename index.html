How Teer Scoring Works

Teer is a traditional archery-based game regulated in Meghalaya under the Meghalaya Amusements and Betting Tax (Amendment) Act. Matches are organized by local archery clubs such as the Khasi Hills Archery Sports Association (KHASA) from Monday to Saturday, with Sundays closed.

First Round (FR) & Second Round (SR)

In the First Round, 50 archers each shoot 30 arrows at a cylindrical bamboo target roughly 15–20 meters away. Once shooting stops, officials count every arrow embedded in the target. The last two digits of that total become the FR result (for example, 1,148 arrows equals 48). In the Second Round, each archer shoots 20 arrows, and the last two digits of the second count form the SR result.
